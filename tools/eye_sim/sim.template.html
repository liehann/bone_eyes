Bone Eyes Simulator

Both 160×160 GC9D01 eyes, as the skull shows them to a visitor. The pixels come from the firmware renderer's tables and maths; the motion is a port of the firmware's eye task.

Right eye · viewer's left · CS 15
Left eye · viewer's right · CS 5
Mood

Moods set the lid opening and pupil size the controller eases toward. Blinks also fire on their own every 2–6 s.

Artwork

Actual size is 18 mm across. Its accuracy depends on your screen's DPI.

Hold a gaze

Drag in the circle to hold the gaze, like eyes look x y. Right is the viewer's right.

Visitor (planned tracking)

The LD2410C reports distance only. Position stands in for the faked direction. Closer visitors make the eyes converge.

The renderer here is checked against the C++ one when the page is built, using pixel checksums over several poses for both artworks. The browser shows RGB565 expanded to 8 bits, so expect the real IPS panel's gamma and brightness to differ.

Built by tools/eye_sim/build.py. Edit src/eye_render.h or src/eyes.cpp, then mirror the change in eye_core.js and rebuild.
